# Quality Department System - User Manual

Prepared by MIS Department, Imo Chicken & Agro (Pvt) Ltd.

---

## 1. Getting started

- Open the system in **Chrome** or **Edge**.
- Log in with your username and password. The menu shows only the pages your user is allowed to use.
- Use the **Logout** button at the top right of any page to sign out.
- If your session expires, the system takes you back to the login page. Log in again.

## 2. Screen layout

- **Sidebar (left):** the menu. Click a group name (CCP Control, Reports, KPI) to open or close it. The group of the page you are on opens automatically.
- **Header (top):** the page title, the current date and time, the light / dark **theme** switch and **Logout**.
- **Filter row:** choose a date or month, then press **LOAD REPORT**. The export icon opens **Export PDF** and **Export CSV**.
- **Bottom bar:** the copyright line moves across the bottom of the page.
- After an update, press **Ctrl + Shift + R** to reload the page and clear the old version.

## 3. Who can see what

- **Admin:** all reports, all KPI pages and **User Control**.
- **Owner:** all divisions in Operational Inspection.
- **Division users:** the input pages and the Operational Inspection checklists of their own divisions.
- If a page is missing from your menu, ask the MIS Department to check your access.

## 4. Entering data

1. Choose the date (or month) on the page.
2. Fill every required field (marked with *).
3. Press **Save**. A confirmation box shows what will be saved. Check it and confirm.
4. A success message appears when the data is saved.

**Input pages:** Slaughter Data, EV Data, Birds Hanging Time, Birds Quality Data, Water Plant Data, Packing Data, Sales Returns, Shop Returns, Customer Complaint, Operational Inspection, Sample Parameters, In-Process Rejection, Cool Room Rejection, Personal Hygiene.

### Operational Inspection

- Choose the date and the division. The checklist loads.
- Three tabs: **Pre-Operational**, **Operational** and **Post-Operational**.
- For each item choose **Acceptable** (tick) or **Not Acceptable** (cross).
- For every cross, write the **Action Taken** before saving.
- Press **Save**. Every item must have a status before saving.

## 5. Reports (Admin)

Reports are opened from the **Reports** group in the sidebar.

1. Choose the date or month.
2. Press **LOAD REPORT**.
3. Check the table. Use **Export PDF** or **Export CSV** from the export icon if you need a file.

**Reports:** CCP Control Report, Birds Hanging Time, Quality Report, Water Plant Report, Sales Returns Report, Sales Return Summary, Shop Returns Report, In-Process Rejection Report, Cool Room Rejection Report, Sales Return Rejection Report, Shop Return Rejection Report, Hygiene Fail Report, Customer Complaint Report, Sample Parameters Report, Operational Report, Internal Non Conformance.

### Internal Non Conformance

- The table shows the non-conformance values by month. Some columns are grouped into three sections: Food Safety & Quality Control, Operational & GMP Control, and Environmental & Utility Control.
- **Double-click** a value cell to open a box beside it. The box shows the date, the value, the section, the expected value, the result (OK or INC triggered) and the INC trigger text.
- Click the red **x** in the box, or click outside it, to close it.
- The **STANDARDS** icon button opens the list of the official standards that govern these limits.

## 6. KPI pages

### KPI Overview

- Choose the month at the top. The tiles show the value of each KPI for that month.
- Each tile shows the KPI value, **Achieved** or **Not Achieved**, the standard, and the figures behind the value.
- A tile shows **No Data** when there is no day count for the month.
- Click a tile to open that KPI.
- The month you choose stays selected while you move between the KPI pages in the same browser tab.

### KPI pages (KPI 1 to KPI 7)

- The four cards show the status, the KPI value, the standard and the variance.
- The **progress bar** shows where the value stands against the standard. Its colour changes as the value moves towards the standard.
- The **formula** box shows how the value was calculated.
- The **daily** and **monthly** charts show the trend. Move the mouse over a point to see its figures.

**Standards**

| KPI | Name | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| KPI 1 | Sampling Compliance Rate | above 95% |
| KPI 2 | Food Safety Customer Complaint Rate | at or below 1% |
| KPI 3 | Rate of Meat Rejection | at or below 0.5% |
| KPI 4 | Hygiene Compliance Rate | at or above 98% |
| KPI 5 | Processing Time Calculation | at or below 1% |
| KPI 6 | Operational Compliance Rate | at or above 95% |
| KPI 7 | Internal Audit Compliance Rate | above 95% |

## 7. User Control (Admin)

- Add a new user, edit a user, reset a password, or change a user's access to reports.
- Set a user as active or inactive.
- Keep passwords private. Do not share your login with anyone else.

## 8. Tips and common problems

- **A page shows old information:** press Ctrl + Shift + R.
- **Load button does nothing:** check that the date or month is selected, then try again.
- **Save is not accepted:** read the message on the page. A missing field or an action text is the usual cause.
- **Export does not download:** allow pop-up and multiple downloads for this site in the browser.
- **Night / light mode:** use the theme switch in the header.
- **Something is wrong or missing:** tell the MIS Department with the page name, the date and a screenshot.
